## Our Mission
Welcome! *Devotion Room* is dedicated to creating word-for-word scripture songs designed to help you memorize, meditate on, and hide God's Word in your heart. 

Whether you are looking for a peaceful background for your personal devotion time or an engaging way to memorize scripture, we are glad you are here.
