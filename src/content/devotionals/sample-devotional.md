It’s easy to say we trust God when everything is going perfectly. The real test of our faith happens when we encounter the unexpected—a sudden change in plans, an illness, or a difficult conversation. 

Proverbs 3:5 challenges us not to rely on our "own understanding." Our view of the world is limited. We only see the current chapter, but God sees the entire story. He knows how the piece of the puzzle you are holding right now fits into the beautiful picture He is creating.

Today, whatever challenge you are facing, take a moment to intentionally place it in God's hands. Walking in faith doesn't mean you have all the answers; it means you know the One who does.
