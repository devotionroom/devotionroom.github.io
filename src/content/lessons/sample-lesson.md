## Teaching Outline
1. **Introduction:** What is armor? Why do soldiers wear it?
2. **The Belt of Truth:** Knowing what is true according to God's Word.
3. **The Breastplate of Righteousness:** Living a life that honors God.
4. **Shoes of the Gospel of Peace:** Being ready to share the good news.
5. **The Shield of Faith:** Trusting God when things are hard.
6. **The Helmet of Salvation:** Knowing we belong to Jesus.
7. **The Sword of the Spirit:** Using the Bible to fight lies.

## Activities
- **Armor Relay:** Split the class into teams. Have them race to put on oversized items representing each piece of armor (e.g., a belt, a large jacket for the breastplate, oversized shoes, a cardboard shield, a hat, and a foam sword).
- **Truth vs. Lie:** Call out statements. If it's a truth from God's Word, students stand up. If it's a lie, they sit down.

## Object Lesson
Bring a raincoat and an umbrella. Have a student put them on while you gently sprinkle them with water from a spray bottle. Explain that just as the raincoat and umbrella protect us from the rain, God's armor protects us from spiritual attacks.

## Prayer
"Dear God, thank you for giving us everything we need to stand strong. Help us to put on your armor every day so we can follow you and be protected. In Jesus' name, Amen."
