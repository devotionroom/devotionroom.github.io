## Reflection

Isaiah 41:10 is one of the Bible's most comforting promises for anyone facing fear, uncertainty, or difficult circumstances. God does not simply tell His people not to be afraid—He gives the reason why. His presence goes with them wherever they are. He promises to strengthen them when they are weak, to help them when they cannot help themselves, and to uphold them with His righteous right hand.

These words remind us that our confidence is not found in our own abilities but in God's unchanging character. Whatever challenges you may be facing today, the Lord invites you to place your trust in Him instead of your fears. His strength is greater than your weakness, His peace is greater than your anxiety, and His faithfulness never fails.

As you listen to this scripture song, allow God's Word to settle into your heart. Let these promises replace fear with faith, discouragement with hope, and uncertainty with confidence in the One who is always with you.
