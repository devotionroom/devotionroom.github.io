## Reflection

Psalm 100 is an invitation for every person to worship the Lord with joy. Rather than approaching God out of obligation or fear, this psalm calls us to come before Him with thanksgiving, praise, and confidence in His goodness. It reminds us that God is our Creator, our Shepherd, and the One whose faithful love never ends.

Whether you are celebrating a season of blessing or walking through difficult circumstances, these words encourage you to fix your eyes on the unchanging character of God. His goodness, steadfast love, and faithfulness remain true for every generation.

Take a few moments today to sing these verses, thank God for His faithfulness, and enter His presence with a grateful heart.
