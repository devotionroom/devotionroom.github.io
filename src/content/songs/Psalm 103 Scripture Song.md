## Reflection

Psalm 103:1–5 is a heartfelt call to worship that begins with a personal invitation: "Bless the LORD, O my soul." David reminds himself—and us—not to forget the many benefits the Lord has graciously given. God forgives our sins, heals our diseases, redeems our lives from destruction, crowns us with steadfast love and compassion, and satisfies us with good things so that our strength is renewed.

This passage encourages us to cultivate a thankful heart by remembering God's faithfulness. When we intentionally reflect on His goodness, praise naturally follows. Rather than focusing on our circumstances, Psalm 103 directs our attention to the Lord's character and His unfailing care for His people.

As you listen to this scripture song, take time to remember how God has worked in your own life. Let these inspired words renew your gratitude, strengthen your faith, and draw you into joyful worship. May your soul continually bless the Lord and never forget His abundant goodness.
