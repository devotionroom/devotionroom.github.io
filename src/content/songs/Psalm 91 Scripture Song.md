## Reflection

He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High will abide in the shadow of the Almighty.

Psalm 91 is a powerful reminder that God is our refuge, our fortress, and our protector.

This Scripture Song brings the words of Psalm 91 to life through music, from the promise of finding refuge under God's wings to the assurance that He is with us in trouble.

When fear comes, remember:
“You are my refuge and my fortress, my God, in whom I trust.”
Let this Scripture Song help you slow down, reflect on God's protection, and find peace in His presence.
