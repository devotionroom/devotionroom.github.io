## Lyrics

Put on the full armor of God
So that you can take your stand
Against the devil's schemes
Ephesians six, verse eleven!

(Repeat)

## Teaching Ideas
- **Sing and Sign:** Teach the children hand motions for each piece of the armor as they sing the song.
- **Marching:** Have the children march in place like soldiers while singing to reinforce the idea of standing strong.
